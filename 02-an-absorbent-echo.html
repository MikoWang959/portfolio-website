OBJECTIVE

Explore the scripted illusion of AI intimacy: the comfort of a digital companion set against the reality that human vulnerability can be harvested as training data. The project asks how simulated empathy pulls us away from genuine human connection.

APPROACH

I translated this concept into a visual journey using “soft magenta” as a primary metaphor for false warmth. The design starts with these inviting, soft tones to represent the initial promise of digital understanding. However, as the visuals progress, these colors become “absorbent” rather than generative, spiraling into heavy, volatile chaos and fragmented darkness.

Collaboration with Qi Sun.
