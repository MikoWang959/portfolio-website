OBJECTIVE

Create a visual identity for DesignThinkers 2018, a design conference exploring creativity and diverse perspectives. The system responds to the year’s theme, “Speak The Truth.”

APPROACH

The identity treats the conference as an unknown journey through a maze. Magenta dots trace individual routes through its intersections, turning the idea of choosing your own path into a flexible visual system.
