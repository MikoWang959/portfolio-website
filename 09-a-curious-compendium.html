OBJECTIVE

The goal is to showcase the "behind-the-scenes" of my creative process. This space serves as a digital laboratory for personal experiments, technical deep-dives, and visual studies that exist outside the boundaries of a formal client brief.

APPROACH

I start by pinpointing a design "friction point," like the user flow for a luxury eyewear brand, to explore non-traditional visual solutions. This involves rapid ideation paired with focused execution in various creative tools, allowing me to hone my technical skills. By viewing these studies as stress tests, I merge creative instinct with functional application, transforming spontaneous ideas into a curated library of design possibilities.
