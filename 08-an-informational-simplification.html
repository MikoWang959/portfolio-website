OBJECTIVE

Choose a company and redesign its annual report or corporate sustainability report, and make it easy to identify. Build the overall visual system to make it legible and complete. Show design skills by using different hierarchies, scales, images, and typography.

APPROACH

I redesigned the annual report for the world’s largest food and beverage company, Nestlé. In the annual report, I decided to use orange color to connect with the brand’s positive image and keep the report consistent. I designed and organized a clean layout to display essential financials and information about the company. This project approaches basic typographic principles and explores new innovative ways to communicate with text and images.
