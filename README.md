# Liying Wang Portfolio

A responsive, single-page portfolio using white and black in light and dark modes, with #8B1540 for hover highlights, active project selection, and the scroll cue. It includes a continuous ticker, a drifting hero glow, a new design statement, enlarged About typography, and an image-led selected-work list.

## Preview

Open `index.html` in a browser. Project and portrait images load from the existing portfolio's Wix media domain; display fonts load from Google Fonts.

## Interactions

- Switch between light and dark themes; the choice is saved in the browser.
- Open the full-screen navigation from the hamburger menu.
- Hover or focus a project title to change the full image preview; click the title or preview image to open that project.
- Hover over “Visual Stories” or “Matter” to fill the outlined words with the highlight color.
- Hover the design statement to sweep the outline phrase into burgundy.
- On desktop, a smaller left-facing wand cursor has a star at its tip and leaves a larger fading trail along the pointer path; the hero glow drifts continuously. Both respect reduced-motion settings.
- The hero and footer show San Leandro, California coordinates.
- The moving type band repeats “Made with curiosity,” “Strategy meets form,” and “Visual stories.”
- Use scroll progress and the back-to-top control.
- Utility labels stay at least 11px for easier reading on small screens.

Publishing is unavailable because Sites is disabled for this workspace. The site package is ready for local review and hosting elsewhere.


The site includes a dedicated About page at `about.html` with profile, experience, education, skills, awards, features, and a client-logo grid. Client marks load as brand favicons from their corresponding websites through Google’s favicon image service.
The dedicated Contact page at `contact.html` includes location, email, social links, a mentor-session link, and a message form. The form opens the visitor’s email app with the message addressed to Liying; automatic in-page delivery needs a form backend.
