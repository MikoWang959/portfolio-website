SELECTED WORK  /  03BRANDING

A CRAFTED
EQUILIBRIUM.

OBJECTIVE

Build a visual identity for Bitter + Sweet, a craft coffee destination that balances traditional roasting expertise with contemporary warmth. The brand pairs a premium, minimal look with an approachable character inspired by high-quality coffee.

APPROACH

A double-read monogram brings together a structured B and an open S to express coffee’s bitter and sweet notes. Espresso Brown and Sage Green soften precise geometry, while rounded typography gives the identity a modern craft feel.
