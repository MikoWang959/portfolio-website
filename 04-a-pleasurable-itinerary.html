OBJECTIVE

Design a monthly magazine for the de Young Museum, bringing together a cover, a director’s letter, feature articles and an events calendar. Typography leads the layouts, with a single typeface used throughout.

APPROACH

Titled On Exhibit, the magazine brings color to the museum’s contemporary visual language. Three artists shape distinct chapters through color and typography, while recurring visual cues connect the pages and guide readers through the issue.
