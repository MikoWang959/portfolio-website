OBJECTIVE

Brand an upcoming exhibition for the artist you chose at an art museum. Design a single poster and a book to raise awareness about the exhibition.

APPROACH

I selected the work of the German installation artist Angela Glajcar as the exhibition object. Most of her installations use paper as the main material to reflect the tension and power of the paper. I named the exhibition Dialogue with Paper, and used undulating lines to simulate paper suspended in the air to form the main message of the poster. In the book introducing the exhibition works, I use a serif typeface to highlight the clean and elegant style.
